Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This policy describes how the Neo Agent desktop application handles your data.

Data accessed

With your explicit consent via Google's OAuth consent screen, the application can access:

  • Gmail — reading, organizing, and sending email on your behalf
  • Google Calendar — reading and creating events
  • Google Drive — searching, uploading, and managing files
  • Google Sheets and Google Docs — creating and editing documents
  • Contacts — reading contact information

How data is stored

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on your machine only. Email, calendar, and file content is processed in memory to carry out your requests and is not persisted by the application beyond what you explicitly save.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The application operates solely for the account owner's personal use on their own hardware.
